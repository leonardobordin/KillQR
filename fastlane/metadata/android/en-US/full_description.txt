Scan and create QR codes and barcodes with a private, offline-first Android app.
No account, ads, analytics, or telemetry.

FEATURES
* Scan with the camera, from images and PDF pages, or from embedded images in
  modern Office documents.
* Scan multiple codes at once or keep scanning continuously.
* Create QR codes and supported linear barcodes.
* Keep an optional local history with search, tags, notes, and favorites.
* Import and export validated JSON and CSV files when you choose.
* Choose from light, dark, and AMOLED themes and 8 app languages.

Scans and history stay on the device. The F-Droid build does not request
Internet access; updates are delivered through F-Droid. Camera access is
requested only while scanning. External links and actions require confirmation.
